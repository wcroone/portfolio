Goal

  • Evaluate tensioning methods to improve reliability, adjustability, and packaging while maintaining manufacturability.

Inputs / Constraints

  • Packaging, alignment, serviceability, manufacturing complexity, and expected torque/load envelope.

Toe Link Mount Load-Path Review

Result: Supported redesign decision for improved loading scenario and reduced unwanted compliance.

Suspension Load paths Compliance Design reviews
Click to expand

What “Compliance” Means

  • Compliance is elastic deflection in links/mounts/bearings under load that can change toe/camber dynamically.
  • Reducing unwanted compliance improves stability and predictability by keeping alignment closer to target under braking/cornering.

Goal

  • Improve toe-link mount geometry/load path to reduce deflection and improve suspension behavior under load.

Contribution

  • Aided decision-making for redesign of toe link mounts to uprights to improve loading scenario and compliance behavior.
